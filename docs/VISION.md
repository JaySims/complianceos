# ComplianceOS Vision

## Mission

To build Africa's leading AI-powered digital trust platform for SMEs.

## Vision

Every small business should be able to become trusted, compliant, discoverable, and investment-ready through one intelligent platform.

## Problems We Solve

- Limited business visibility
- Lack of trust
- Compliance complexity
- Poor access to opportunities
- Weak digital presence

## Core Features

- AI Compliance Advisor
- Business Verification
- SME Directory
- Trust Score
- Opportunity Matching
- Business Analytics
- Funding Readiness
